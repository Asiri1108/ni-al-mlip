FINAL ACCEPTANCE CRITERION - NUMERIC THRESHOLD LOCKED 2026-08-17
(revised same day -- see CORRECTION HISTORY below; this supersedes an
earlier same-day locked value of 3.0402 meV/atom, which was found to rest
on a disqualified point before it was ever used to unseal anything)

  STATUS: LOCKED. Will NOT be revised after cfg109/cfg110 are unsealed --
  revising a threshold after seeing the sealed result it is meant to
  judge would defeat the entire pre-registration discipline this file
  exists to enforce.

  METRIC: relative-energy error, meV/atom, same formula used throughout
  this project: (E_pred(config) - E_pred(phase_relaxed)) / natoms,
  compared against the equivalent DFT-derived quantity.

  THRESHOLD: 2.3865 meV/atom

  BASIS: the MAX absolute relative-energy error among the 19 GENUINELY
  INDEPENDENT members of the Dataset-100 TEST+BLIND_HOLDOUT reserved-20,
  evaluating the FROZEN al3ni_combined220_lora_v1 checkpoint (fresh
  leakage check re-run: 0/20 reserved config_ids found in combined-220's
  TRAIN-182 or VALIDATION-18). "Genuinely independent" excludes
  cfg043_Al3Ni_volume_rattle_expansion -- see CFG043 EXCLUSION RATIONALE
  below. The value belongs to cfg060_Al3Ni5_volume_rattle_expansion
  (BLIND_HOLDOUT). Evidence: results/combined220_reserved_evaluation_v1/
  combined220_reserved_per_config.csv,
  configs/COMBINED220_RESERVED_EVALUATION_STATUS.txt,
  sha256=259667ca61783d1e4985ca219cdd93754f39701ab24a18e6b90d2c4bd3940f82.
  This is still a real observed extreme, not a computed percentile or a
  round number -- same derivation philosophy as the leakage threshold
  0.734644 and gate (d) ceiling/floor 0.628835/0.297055 in
  configs/SESSION_STATE_AL3NI_EXPANSION_DESIGN.md Section 3 -- with the
  one change being which population that extreme is drawn from.

  CFG043 EXCLUSION RATIONALE (explicit, not silent):
  cfg043_Al3Ni_volume_rattle_expansion IS a genuine BLIND_HOLDOUT member
  of Dataset-100 by the frozen manifest (verified directly, not assumed:
  data/datasets/ni_al_dataset100_blind_holdout_manifest.csv row 83,
  split=blind_holdout, target_role=BLIND_HOLDOUT) -- it was never
  conflated with the set, it is a real member of it. It is excluded from
  THRESHOLD-SETTING use specifically because it is design-contaminated:
  it has been evaluated at every single checkpoint in this project since
  round 1 (hard-coded as REF_IDS[0] in
  scripts/evaluate_al3ni_interim_gate_{127,129,211,218}.py), and every
  remediation round's design was explicitly steered by driving its error
  down (configs/NI_AL_DATA_SHOWCASE.md Section 11: 6.60 -> 2.88 -> 2.15
  meV/atom, tracked and acted on round by round). It was never
  gradient-trained on -- no direct TRAIN leakage -- but it has functioned
  as a de facto human-in-the-loop validation signal across 5+ design
  rounds. That is a real, documented form of INDIRECT leakage through the
  design process, distinct from direct training leakage, and it
  disqualifies cfg043 from anchoring a single-point MAX-based threshold
  (though it remains a legitimate, undisturbed member of the reserved-20
  for aggregate reporting, where one point's influence is diluted rather
  than determinative).

  SENSITIVITY TABLE (supporting evidence, |relative-energy error|,
  meV/atom, frozen combined-220 checkpoint):

    | Population        | n  | min    | median | p75    | p90    | max              |
    |--------------------|----|--------|--------|--------|--------|------------------|
    | WITH cfg043        | 20 | 0.0197 | 0.2499 | 0.9799 | 2.2131 | 3.0402 (cfg043)  |
    | WITHOUT cfg043     | 19 | 0.0197 | 0.2474 | 0.9202 | 1.5907 | 2.3865 (cfg060)  |

  Dropping the one contaminated point moves max by 22% (3.0402 ->
  2.3865) and p90 by 28% (2.2131 -> 1.5907); median/p75 barely move.
  The distortion is concentrated entirely in the upper tail -- exactly
  where a max-based threshold reads from -- which is direct quantitative
  confirmation that cfg043 does not belong in the derivation basis.

  AGGREGATE STABILITY CONTEXT: despite cfg043 itself regressing across
  the two most recent (small, noise-prone) rounds -- 2.046 meV/atom
  (combined-211) -> 2.681 (combined-218, +7 configs) -> 3.040
  (combined-220, +2 configs), matching the established
  small-round-regresses-probes pattern exactly, no exception -- the
  AGGREGATE held-out-20 relative-energy error actually IMPROVED slightly
  over the same span: RMSE 1.148 -> 1.129 meV/atom, MAE 0.763 -> 0.713
  meV/atom (combined-211 evaluation:
  configs/COMBINED211_RESERVED_EVALUATION_STATUS.txt; combined-220:
  configs/COMBINED220_RESERVED_EVALUATION_STATUS.txt). This is consistent
  with cfg043's rise being small-round noise riding on a stable-to-
  improving underlying model, not a real generalization failure.

  EXPLICIT LIMITATION -- confidence bound, not just a caveat: only TWO
  full reserved-20 evaluations exist in this project's history
  (combined-211 and combined-220). The "aggregate is stable" claim above
  rests on ONE before/after comparison, not a trend line -- there is no
  dense time series for the full-20 aggregate the way there is for
  cfg043 alone. Treat the stability claim, and by extension this
  threshold, as resting on limited longitudinal evidence, not as
  statistically well-established over multiple independent checkpoints.

  DOCUMENTED FALLBACK (not the primary basis): p90 of the held-out-19
  (excluding cfg043) = 1.5907 meV/atom. Less sensitive to any single
  remaining point than a max statistic at this small sample size (n=19),
  at the cost of moving further from the project's "real accepted
  extreme, not a computed percentile" precedent. Recorded here for
  reference; NOT the locked threshold. If a future review finds the
  max-of-19 basis too fragile, this is the pre-registered next candidate
  -- not to be adopted after the fact without being written down first.

  JUSTIFICATION (one sentence): 2.3865 meV/atom is the worst observed
  relative-energy error among the 19 reserved held-out configurations
  that have never been read, trained on, or used to steer any design
  decision in this project (cfg060), explicitly excluding the one
  reserved point (cfg043) with documented indirect design-process
  leakage, and it remains ~8.9x tighter than the ~21.228 meV/atom
  zero-shot baseline (configs/NI_AL_DATA_SHOWCASE.md Section 7,
  `AlNi_iso_m02`) that originally defined "unacceptable" for this
  project.

  RULE: at unsealing, compute the same relative-energy error for
  cfg109_Al3Ni_iso_expansion and cfg110_Al3Ni_volume_rattle_expansion
  against al3ni_combined220_lora_v1. PASS requires BOTH configs' absolute
  relative-energy error <= 2.3865 meV/atom. This is a per-config
  threshold, not an average -- consistent with using a max (worst-case
  among the clean 19), not a mean, as the derivation basis.

  cfg115 (VALIDATION-role, seen during training-time model selection, not
  reserved data) remains explicitly excluded from this threshold's
  evidence, same as before. cfg043's own relative-energy error at
  unsealing time should still be RECORDED for continuity with its time
  series, but must NOT be used to judge pass/fail -- it is not part of
  the threshold basis.

  CORRECTION HISTORY: this file originally locked 3.0402 meV/atom (the
  max INCLUDING cfg043) on 2026-08-17, in direct contradiction of the
  standing instruction that cfg043/cfg115 were "context only, not
  threshold basis." The error was caught the same day, before any
  unsealing occurred, via explicit review of cfg043's true split role and
  design-contamination history (Sections above). No unsealing was ever
  performed against the incorrect value.

INTERIM GATE - HISTORICAL (110-STRUCTURE THROUGH COMBINED-218 CHECKPOINTS)
NOT SEALED. NO PASS/FAIL THRESHOLD APPLIED AT THE TIME. SUPERSEDED BY THE
LOCKED THRESHOLD ABOVE FOR ALL FUTURE (I.E. UNSEALING) DECISIONS.

  Purpose (as originally written, kept verbatim for history): track
  whether each remediation round was moving the model in the right
  direction, without touching sealed data and without forcing a premature
  go/no-go decision.

  Metrics tracked, non-sealed, every round from 110 through combined-218:

  1. Relative-energy error on cfg043_Al3Ni_volume_rattle_expansion
     (Dataset-100 HOLDOUT, NOT sealed) - each new checkpoint vs the prior
     one. This is the same comparison originally used to diagnose the
     cfg043 failure (configs/SESSION_STATE_AL3NI_EXPANSION_DESIGN.md
     Section 2).

  2. Relative-energy error on cfg115_Al3Ni_iso_expansion (VALIDATION, NOT
     sealed; this session's resolved +5.30% design,
     configs/SESSION_STATE_AL3NI_EXPANSION_DESIGN.md Section 6/7).

  These trend numbers (per SESSION_STATE_AL3NI_EXPANSION_DESIGN.md Section
  13's decision log) are exactly what showed round-size-correlated swings
  and fed the 2026-08-17 stop-expansion decision -- they were never a
  pass/fail gate, and still are not. The FINAL ACCEPTANCE CRITERION above,
  not these two probes, is what unsealing is judged against.

AL3NI ROUND285 SEALED PAIR (cfg297/cfg299) - ACCEPTANCE CRITERION
NUMERIC THRESHOLD LOCKED 2026-08-18

STATUS: LOCKED. Will NOT be revised after cfg297/cfg299 are unsealed --
revising a threshold after seeing the sealed result it is meant to judge
would defeat the entire pre-registration discipline this file exists to
enforce (same rule as AL3NI_REMEDIATION_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERION.txt for
cfg109/cfg110). This file was written, and cfg297/cfg299 remain fully
sealed, before it is applied to anything.

BACKGROUND
cfg109/cfg110 (combined-220's confirmation-holdout pair) were unsealed
2026-08-17 against al3ni_combined220_lora_v1 using a threshold (2.3865
meV/atom) derived from a SINGLE seed's reserved-20 distribution. A later
seed-variance investigation found that single-seed threshold was fragile:
across seeds 20260811/812/813, the reserved-19 (excl. cfg043) max moved
0.808 meV/atom and its anchoring config changed identity (cfg060 ->
cfg075 -> cfg060) -- a real weakness in how that threshold was derived,
even though it did not change the FAIL verdict on cfg109/cfg110 itself.
Round285 replaced cfg109/cfg110 with a new confirmation-holdout pair
(cfg297_Al3Ni_iso_expansion, s=3.5%; cfg299_Al3Ni_volume_rattle_expansion,
s=3.6%) after retraining on combined-227 (combined-220 + round285's 7 new
TRAIN structures) produced a SUCCESS verdict against the pre-registered
round285 success criteria (configs/ROUND285_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.txt): cfg109
error dropped 3.7671->2.2674 meV/atom (delta +1.4997), cfg110 dropped
3.7320->2.2420 (delta +1.4900), both deltas ~3.5x the measured 0.43
meV/atom noise floor.

This file derives the acceptance threshold cfg297/cfg299 will be judged
against at their own future unsealing event, applying BOTH lessons from
the combined-220 threshold's history:

LESSON 1 -- EXCLUDE cfg043 FROM THE DERIVATION BASIS
cfg043 is design-contaminated: it has been evaluated at every checkpoint
in this project since round 1 and every remediation round's design was
explicitly steered by driving its error down (see
AL3NI_REMEDIATION_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERION.txt, CFG043 EXCLUSION RATIONALE,
for the full argument -- unchanged and still binding here). It remains a
legitimate member of the reserved-20 for aggregate reporting, but is
excluded from anchoring a threshold. Basis population: reserved-19
(Dataset-100 TEST(5)+BLIND_HOLDOUT(15) minus cfg043).

LESSON 2 -- DO NOT DERIVE FROM A SINGLE SEED
ROUTE TAKEN: retrained combined-227 twice more (seed 20260812, seed
20260813; identical data, identical hyperparameters -- LoRA rank 4, lr
0.001, 100 epochs/patience 20 -- to the existing seed-20260811 checkpoint,
seed changed only), rather than widening a single-seed threshold by the
0.43 meV/atom noise floor.

WHY THIS ROUTE, NOT THE NOISE-FLOOR-WIDENING FALLBACK: retraining was
already proven cheap in this exact project -- the equivalent combined-220
seed-variance pair (seeds 812/813) completed in under 20 minutes total on
a single GPU, and this round's two additional combined-227 runs completed
in comparable time (seed 812: ~10 min; seed 813: ~10 min; both confirmed
complete via configs/ROUND285_SEALED_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERION derivation run,
results/round285_sealed_threshold_v1/console.log). At that cost, actually
measuring the multi-seed distribution is strictly better evidence than
inflating a single-seed number by a flat noise-floor margin, which would
still not tell us whether the anchoring config is stable across seeds --
exactly the property that broke down last time (cfg060 -> cfg075 ->
cfg060). The noise-floor-widening fallback was not needed and was not
used.

DERIVATION
For each of seeds {20260811, 20260812, 20260813} (identical combined-227
data/hyperparameters, seed-only variation), computed the |relative-energy
error| -- (E_pred(config) - E_pred(phase_relaxed)) / natoms, vs the
DFT-derived equivalent, same formula used throughout this project -- for
all 19 reserved-19 members, and took each seed's own maximum:

  seed 20260811: max = 2.5232 meV/atom (cfg060_Al3Ni5_volume_rattle_expansion)
  seed 20260812: max = 2.4333 meV/atom (cfg060_Al3Ni5_volume_rattle_expansion)
  seed 20260813: max = 2.6183 meV/atom (cfg060_Al3Ni5_volume_rattle_expansion)

  Spread across 3 seeds: 0.1850 meV/atom (vs. 0.8080 for the combined-220
  single-seed-derived threshold's equivalent cross-seed spread) -- a real
  improvement in stability, and the anchoring config (cfg060) is IDENTICAL
  across all 3 seeds this time, unlike the prior derivation where it
  changed identity. Not assumed to generalize further without evidence;
  recorded as observed for this specific model/data combination.

  Full per-seed distribution (reserved-19, meV/atom):
    seed 20260811: min=0.0035 median=0.2662 p75=0.9297 p90=1.6306 max=2.5232
    seed 20260812: min=0.0016 median=0.2958 p75=0.8253 p90=1.7838 max=2.4333
    seed 20260813: min=0.0048 median=0.3030 p75=1.0184 p90=1.5013 max=2.6183

THRESHOLD: 2.6183 meV/atom

BASIS: the MAX of the three seeds' own per-seed maxima over the reserved-19
(19 genuinely independent Dataset-100 TEST+BLIND_HOLDOUT members, cfg043
excluded), evaluated on combined-227 (TRAIN 189/VALIDATION 18) under seeds
20260811/20260812/20260813 -- n=19 configs x n=3 seeds. Locking the
cross-seed MAX (not the mean) means the threshold is set so that ALL
THREE tested seeds' own worst reserved point would pass it -- robust to
whichever seed happens to produce the model actually used at unsealing
time, not just the one seed that happened to train first. This mirrors
the original derivation philosophy (a real observed extreme sets the
bar, not a computed percentile or invented round number), extended across
seeds the way the original was only extended across configs.
